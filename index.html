Panic Loop Monitor (prototype)

Client-side prototype that pulls public RSS headlines from major outlets and sorts them into four categories based on language patterns associated with panic framing.
Cat 1: current panic mongering likely · Cat 2: has panic-inducing traits · Cat 3: clickbaitish · Cat 4: worth being aware of.
Does not assess truth, causation, or official statements. Only surfaces potential framing. No server, no cost.
