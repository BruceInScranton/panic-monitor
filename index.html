Panic Loop Monitor (prototype)

Client-side prototype that pulls public RSS headlines from major outlets and flags items matching keywords associated with the kinds of clustered technical or aerial reports that can feed panic loops (outages, drones, nuclear, blackouts, etc.).
It does not assess truth, causation, or official statements. It only surfaces potential framing. Host anywhere free (GitHub Pages, Netlify, local file). No server, no cost.
